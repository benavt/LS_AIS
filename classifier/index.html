Method and verification

Fit each chain independently to each anchor using 265 Cα atoms outside the ROIs: 1–44, 57–145 and 167–298. Measure Cα RMSE without refitting in PSTAIRE 45–56 and T-loop 146–166. Each ROI score is 100 × RMSE-to-001 / (RMSE-to-001 + RMSE-to-072). Both ROIs nearer 072 → active-like; both nearer 001 → inactive-like; disagreement → mixed; a tie or missing data → indeterminate.

Coverage, exclusions and validation

All 176,712 inputs produced one complete exact-sequence CDK2 chain. Source identities and frozen native hashes were checked before scoring; acceptance verified every output hash, roster membership, saved ROI ratio and label, complete residue maps, and parity for all 284 pilot monomers. Both finished resource logs passed the pinned checker, and worker/launcher exit codes were zero.

Excluded before execution: one previously rejected zero-byte DOC1R input, plus two redundant top-ranked AFsample3 summary files outside the accepted 1,000 seed/sample models per target. The 001/072 anchor controls are separate from these population counts. Inactive-like labels require agreement between both ROIs; a low overall score alone is insufficient.
